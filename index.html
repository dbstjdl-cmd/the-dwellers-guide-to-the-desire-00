01 / A QUESTION OF SPACE

Architecture has never been merely a response to necessity.

It is also a reflection of what we fear, what we lack, and what we desire.

Behind every wall, every boundary, and every room lies a decision shaped by the society we inhabit.

02 / THE INVISIBLE DESIRES

We often imagine the future through the scale of global crises.

Yet the concerns closest to our everyday lives are frequently smaller, quieter, and deeply personal.

Loneliness. Recognition. Belonging. Insecurity.

What if these invisible desires are already shaping the spaces we inhabit?

03 / A SPECULATIVE FUTURE

Speculative architecture does not attempt to predict the future or provide definitive solutions.

Instead, it transforms the contradictions of the present into spatial questions.

What happens when a desire becomes an obsession?

When a social condition is pushed to its extreme?

And when the architecture of everyday life begins to reflect that transformation?
